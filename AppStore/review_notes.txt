IDFA Vault - Notes for App Review

1. SCREEN RECORDING
A screen recording from a physical iPhone running the latest iOS is attached. It starts by launching the app from the Home Screen and shows unlocking with Face ID, this device's IDFA, copying and sharing it, the user's other devices synced through iCloud, settings, and locking the app again.
Screen recording protection: to protect the IDFA, the app covers its screen while the screen is recorded or mirrored ("Hide During Screen Recording or Mirroring", on by default). For this video it was turned off in the app's Settings (the sliders button at the top right). If you record the screen while testing, turn it off before you start recording (while recording, the cover also hides Settings).
On iPhone and iPad, the App Tracking Transparency prompt appears after unlocking while the permission is not yet determined. It is not in the video because tracking was already allowed on this device.
The app has no account registration, login, user-generated content, paid content, or In-App Purchases.

2. PURPOSE AND TARGET AUDIENCE
IDFA Vault shows the device's advertising identifier (IDFA) so that the user can copy it. It is for developers who register test devices in advertising SDKs, and for people who are asked for their IDFA by a carrier or a service.
The IDFA is used only to display it on screen and to save it to the user's own iCloud private database. It is never used for advertising or tracking. The app contains no ads, analytics, or third-party SDKs, and has no server. The developer cannot access any user data.
To protect the identifier, the app always asks for Face ID, Touch ID, or Optic ID (or the device passcode) at launch and when it returns from the background.

3. HOW TO ACCESS THE MAIN FEATURES
No login, account, credentials, or sample files are required.
1. Launch the app and authenticate with Face ID (or the passcode).
2. Allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. The permission is used only to read the IDFA and show it on screen; without it, iOS returns all zeros and the app shows how to allow it in Settings.
3. On the home screen, tap "Copy" to copy the IDFA (it is cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds) or "Share" to share it.
4. Open IDFA Vault on another device signed in with the same Apple Account. Its IDFA appears under "SAME APPLE ID" within about 20 seconds. Tap a row to show all digits.
5. Tap the settings button (top right) to change the auto-lock timing, masking, screen recording protection, and iCloud sync, or to delete all synced data.
On iPad and Mac, the devices are listed in a sidebar and the IDFA can also be shown as a QR code. On Mac, the menu bar item asks for Touch ID or the password each time it opens.
On Mac and Apple Vision Pro, the system does not provide the IDFA to apps (on macOS the tracking status always stays "not determined" and no prompt is shown; the visionOS SDK has no AdSupport framework). These versions show a short explanation instead of their own IDFA, list the IDFAs of the user's iPhone and iPad synced through iCloud (open IDFA Vault on an iPhone or iPad with the same Apple Account to see them), do not show the tracking prompt, and do not write their own record to iCloud.

4. EXTERNAL SERVICES
None other than Apple's CloudKit. Each iPhone and iPad writes one record (an app-generated device UUID, the device model name, the platform, the IDFA, and the tracking permission status) to the user's own iCloud private database, so that the user's devices can show these IDFAs. The public and shared databases are not used. The user can turn sync off or delete all synced data in Settings.

5. REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
None. The app's features and content are the same in all regions.

6. REGULATED INDUSTRY / THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
The app is not in a regulated industry and contains no third-party protected material. The biometric marks are Apple's SF Symbols. No fonts are bundled.
